Privacy Policy
⚠️ Draft — pending legal review. This page describes our intended data practices but has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel or a privacy specialist. Do not treat this as a final, binding policy until that review is complete.

Last updated: Not yet published

Information We Collect

  • Account info: name, email, and authentication data (via Firebase Authentication)
  • Shipping info: address and phone number, if you provide them
  • Subscription and billing info, once payment processing is connected
  • Collection data you choose to enter into your Collection tracker
  • Usage data such as pages visited, for improving the site

How We Use Your Information

To operate your account and subscription, ship your crates, communicate with you (per your Notification settings), and improve CardCrate Club.

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage, and plan to use Stripe for payment processing. These providers have their own privacy practices governing data they process on our behalf.

Your Choices

You can review and update your info anytime from Account Settings, control notification preferences, and request account deletion by contacting us.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, though no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our Contact page.

How It Works

From joining the waitlist to growing a collection you're proud of — here's the full journey.

1

🙋 Join

Create your free account — no credit card required. Founding 100 spots are locked in the moment you subscribe.

2

📦 Choose Crate

Pick the tier that fits your budget and collecting style — Pack Club, Trainer Club, Collector Club, or Box Club.

3

🎯 Set Preferences

Tell us what you love — newest sets, chase cards, sealed products, art cards, or let us surprise you.

4

🔎 We Source Products

Our team sources sealed product directly, so you never compete with scalpers or empty shelves.

5

📮 Pack

Your crate is packed carefully in protective packaging, curated to your preferences.

6

🚚 Ship

Tracked delivery straight to your door, every month — follow progress live from your Dashboard.

7

🪙 Earn Coins

Every crate earns Crate Coins — redeemable in the Rewards Shop for packs, discounts, and exclusives.

8

📈 Grow Your Collection

Track everything in your Collection dashboard and watch your collection — and its value — grow over time.

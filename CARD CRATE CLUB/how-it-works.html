How It Works

A simple path from joining the launch list to receiving curated sealed products and building a collection you can track over time.

1

Join the Waitlist

Reserve your place in the launch queue for free. No credit card is required to join the waitlist. Founding Member status is confirmed when an eligible launch subscription is completed.

2

Choose Your Crate

Compare Pack Club, Trainer Club, Collector Club, and Box Club, then choose the option that best fits your budget and collecting style.

3

Set Your Preferences

Tell us what you like from the sets and sealed products available for that shipment so your crate can be built around your interests.

4

We Source Sealed Product

Card Crate Club sources authentic sealed trading-card products from trusted suppliers and available distribution channels. Product selection can vary by release and availability.

5

Your Crate Is Packed

Products are organized in protective packaging with Card Crate Club inserts so the shipment feels intentional instead of simply thrown into a mailer.

6

Shipment & Tracking

When your paid crate ships, available tracking details can be shown in your account so you can follow the shipment to your door.

7

Earn Crate Coins

Eligible subscriptions and community activities can earn Crate Coins for future rewards, discounts, and member perks as the rewards program launches.

8

Grow Your Collection

Use your collection tools to keep track of cards and sets over time while new subscriber features continue to roll out.

Pre-launch details, product availability, rewards, and fulfillment features may change before the first paid shipment. Final terms will be shown before checkout.

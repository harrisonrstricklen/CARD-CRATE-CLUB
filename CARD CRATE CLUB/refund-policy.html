Refund Policy

Last updated: Not yet published

Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription anytime from your Dashboard. Canceling stops future billing — it does not automatically refund the current billing period.

Mid-Cycle Refunds

Because crates are sourced and prepared ahead of shipment, refunds for a crate that has already shipped are generally not available. If your crate hasn't shipped yet when you cancel, contact us and we'll review your case.

Damaged or Missing Items

If your crate arrives damaged, or an item is missing, contact us with photos and your order details within a reasonable window of delivery. We'll work with you to make it right — a replacement, credit, or refund depending on the situation.

Founding Member Price Lock

Founding Member pricing is locked in for as long as your subscription stays active without a gap; canceling and resubscribing later isn't guaranteed to preserve your original locked rate.

How to Request a Refund

Reach out via our Contact page with your order details and we'll respond within 24 hours.

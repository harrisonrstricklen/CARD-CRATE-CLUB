# Card Crate Club — Official Brand Mark

## Canonical logo asset

The official Card Crate Club emblem is the nested hexagonal triple-C mark stored at:

`CARD CRATE CLUB/brand-mark.svg`

This file is the canonical source for the emblem used throughout the website. Other logo appearances should reference this SVG rather than redraw or reinterpret the mark.

## Brand direction

- Mark: three nested hexagonal C forms opening to the right
- Construction: each C follows a hexagonal structure rather than a rounded arc
- Opening: the right side of each C uses the same angular triangular wedge / 38-degree cut treatment
- Shape: compact and relatively closed, matching the approved packaging-logo direction
- Primary mark color: Card Crate Club Gold `#F5C842`
- Primary dark background: `#0A0A0F`
- Wordmark: `CARD CRATE CLUB`
- Preferred use: gold emblem on black or very dark background
- Do not add a period after CCC or the company name

## Trademark asset control

The hexagonal 38-degree-cut mark was designated by the brand owner as the official Card Crate Club logo direction on August 31, 2026. The repository history preserves the SVG source and subsequent revisions as dated Git commits.

Before filing any trademark application, export the exact version being filed from `brand-mark.svg` and keep that filing specimen unchanged in a dedicated archive. Trademark registration and clearance should be reviewed with a qualified trademark attorney or through the USPTO process.

Shipping Policy
⚠️ Draft — pending legal review. This page describes our intended shipping practices but has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel. Do not treat this as a final, binding policy until that review is complete.

Last updated: Not yet published

Processing Time

Crates are typically packed and prepared for shipment within a few business days of your billing date. Processing times may vary during high-demand periods or new set releases.

Shipping Carriers & Methods

We ship via major carriers with tracked service on every order. Collector Club and Box Club subscribers receive priority shipping; all other tiers include standard free shipping.

Tracking

Once your crate ships, tracking information is available from your Dashboard's shipping timeline and, if enabled, sent to your email.

Delivery Areas

We currently plan to ship within the United States, with international shipping under consideration for the future. Availability by region will be confirmed before launch.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your crate arrives damaged or doesn't arrive, contact us within a reasonable window of the expected delivery date so we can investigate with the carrier.

Questions

Reach out via our Contact page for anything not covered here.

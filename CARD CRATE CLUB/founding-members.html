THE FOUNDING 100

The first 100 qualifying paid subscribers become part of Card Crate Club from the beginning. Founder status is about recognition, access, and experiences we can keep delivering as the club grows—not an unrealistic promise that your subscription price can never change.

Founder numbers are assigned only to eligible paid subscriptions. We’ll display a live remaining-spot count once the production counter is connected to confirmed subscriptions rather than showing a made-up number.

Founding Member Perks
🎟️

One Giveaway Entry for Life

Founders receive one complimentary entry in each eligible Card Crate Club giveaway for life. Every giveaway remains subject to its official rules, eligibility requirements, and applicable law.

🛍️

First Opportunity at New Store Items

Founders get an early shopping window on select new products, limited drops, and other store inventory before general member or public access when available.

🔢

Permanent Founder Number

Your unique #001–#100 number remains part of your Card Crate Club account and founding history.

🎴

Physical Founder Card

A collectible Founding 100 member card created specifically for the original members of the club.

🏅

Founder Profile Badge

A permanent Founding 100 badge on your account so your early-member status is visible as the community grows.

📦

First Look at Future Crate Options

Founders get advance notice when new crate formats, eligible TCGs, or special product options are introduced.

Why there’s no lifetime subscription price lock

Booster pack acquisition costs, distributor pricing, shipping, and product availability can all change. Card Crate Club may need to adjust future subscription pricing to keep the service sustainable. The goal is to give members advance notice of recurring price changes and preserve the Founder benefits above independently from the monthly subscription price.
